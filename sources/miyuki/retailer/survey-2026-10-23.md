# Retail research: Miyuki round rocailles 2039–2060 (market sources)

Started 2026-10-23. Scope: shop listings (Western + Japanese) naming a number 2039–2060 as a Miyuki
round rocaille / seed bead with a size. Images viewed on pages only; nothing downloaded.

Legend: RR = round rocaille; codes: `11-2046` (Miyuki item style), `92046` (US wholesaler "9" prefix),
`SB11-2046` (Miyuki-style seed bead code), `RR11-2046`.

### Method note
WebFetch and curl get HTTP 429 from Shopify stores. Used the built-in browser instead and ran the
store's own `/search/suggest.json?q=<n>` from the page (same-origin fetch), queries `11-<n>`, `<n>`,
`miyuki <n>` (and 8-/15-/6- where noted) for n = 2039..2060. Delica (DB) hits filtered out.

## SUMMARY (market evidence)
- One coherent series: 11/0 round rocailles 2041–2051, "Special Dyed" (opaque, matte, dyed), sold in US/CA/UK/DE/NL/ES/AU shops,
  typically as 22–24 g tubes under codes 11-20xx / 11-920xx(-TB) / SB11-20xxV / MI-11-20xx-5. Not found in Japanese domestic retail
  (Rakuten, Miyuki Factory). John Bead (CA distributor) stocks only 2048.
- Names (consensus): 2041 Pale Pumpkin, 2042 Harvest Orange, 2043 Chocolate Brown (Aura: Brown Orange), 2044 Reddish Brown (Aura: Chocolate),
  2045 Bright Pink, 2046 Old Rose, 2047 Wine (Aura title variant "Antique Rose"), 2048 Hunter Green, 2049 Olive Green, 2050 Bright Turquoise,
  2051 Dark Teal Blue.
- Other sizes: only Bead Worx AU 8-92047 "8/0 Matte Special Dyed Wine" (added Feb 2026; single source). No 15/0, 6/0, 5/0 of 2041–2051 anywhere.
- 2039 = Matte Metallic Royal Blue at 15/0 (Aura, Beadazzle, Bead Abode, Peppy, CJ, aperlen, Bead Worx, Magnolia, Argus multi) and 8/0 (Peppy, CJ).
- 2040 = Matte Metallic Dark Maroon / "Brick Red" at 15/0, 11/0, 8/0, 6/0 (+ Tila/Half Tila 2040).
- 2052–2060: no round-rocaille listing anywhere. Numbers collide with Delica DB2052 Luminous Asparagus, DB2053 Luminous Mermaid Green,
  DB2054 Luminous Dusk Blue (DB2031–DB2054 = Luminous Delicas), Long Magatama LMA-2053 (Matte Gunmetal AB), Preciosa/Czech colour codes (20500 etc.).
