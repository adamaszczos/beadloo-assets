## Sample-card colours not found in any other source (2026-10-16)

The official sample cards (`/wp-json/miyuki/v1/samplecards`, PDFs at `/common/data/pdf/<file>.pdf`) are photographs, so numbers were read by OCR on cell crops and every cell the OCR flagged as new was then checked by eye (the OCR misread some, e.g. `E3255` as `25D`, `#2352` as `2052`). Cells the OCR read as an already-known code were not re-checked, so this list is a lower bound. Cards: `43/2R` (11/0 dyed semi-frosted), `44/2R`, `13/3R` (5/0 E beads), `76/4R` (Rococo), `853/R`, `866R/1-3` (6/0), `867/R`, plus the Black and Mud cover cards. The cards state Round Rocailles sizes, but the Black and Mud cover cards cover several sizes and I did not check that every number on them is a round rocaille.
